Controls & requirement map

Project 08 — Houses from outside · Group 07 · Section C2

Camera — keyboard

  • W S move closer / further from the houses
  • A D orbit left / right around the houses
  • Q E lower / raise the orbit elevation
  • R F raise / lower the look-at point
  • ← → ↑ ↓ pan across the plot
  • Shift hold to move 2.4× faster
  • 12345 camera presets
  • [ ] narrow / widen perspective FOV
  • V reset the view, Space auto orbit tour

Scene — keyboard

  • C next cottage texture (same as clicking it)
  • B switch building facade brick ↔ concrete
  • T pause / resume the day-night cycle
  • Y skip time forward (about 3 hours)
  • L pause / resume the orbiting light
  • O show / hide the orbit path ring
  • K summer ↔ autumn leaves
  • M cycle wind strength
  • X shadow maps on / off
  • P save a PNG snapshot · H hide the HUD

Mouse

  • Left click on the cottage — changes its texture (required interaction)
  • Hover the cottage — blue rim highlight + tooltip
  • Left drag — orbit the camera
  • Right drag — pan the look-at point
  • Wheel — zoom in / out

Snapshots for the report

  • Press 1…5, then P. The PNG lands in your Downloads folder, ready for section 3.2.

Where each requirement is met

  1. Custom shaders — sky dome, building facade, cottage walls, foliage wind, chimney smoke, fresnel glow (js/shaders.js)
  2. Lighting — hemisphere + sun (shadow casting) + orbiting point light + street lamp, with hand-written Blinn-Phong in GLSL (js/lighting.js)
  3. Perspective projection — PerspectiveCamera(55°, 0.1, 1200), live FOV control with [ ]
  4. Texture on every object — 14 procedural tileable sets with normal maps (assets/textures)
  5. Animation — light rotates around the building, plus day-night cycle, wind and smoke
  6. Mouse + keyboard — click the cottage to change texture, keyboard flies the camera around the houses
